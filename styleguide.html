Contrast

WCAG asks for 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text (24px+, or 19px+ bold). Anton headings and buttons count as large.

Hand lettering

Big headings are drawn by hand and used as images, never set in a font. Wrap them in a heading with .lettering, and put the words in the alt text so screen readers and Google can read them.

<h2 class="lettering">
  <img src="assets/images/heading-good-news.webp" width="1100" height="207" alt="The Good News.">
</h2>

Motion

Add data-reveal to any block and it fades up 24px over 0.9s the first time it scrolls into view. Buttons press in over 0.12s. Both switch off for visitors who choose reduced motion.

<section class="section measure" data-reveal> … </section>
